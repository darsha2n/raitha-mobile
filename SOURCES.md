Checked on 9 October 2026. These are named-variety reference figures, not
district averages, personalised crop recommendations, or current-season forecasts.

## Conversion and use

One acre = 0.40468564224 hectares; one quintal = 100 kilograms.
The app preserves the original yield range and unit alongside its converted
quintals-per-acre figures. Computations use the unrounded conversions;
displayed references are rounded to two decimal places.

The planner lets a farmer use a reference bound deliberately or enter their
own expected saleable yield. It shows per-acre and full-area profit from the
farmer's own costs and price, plus two hypothetical reference-bound examples.
Those examples hold price and costs fixed: they are not confidence intervals
or best/worst outcomes. Actual farm yield can lie outside published ranges.

No selling prices or production costs have been invented. No automatic
highest-profit crop recommendation is made. Confirm variety, soil, season,
water availability and market access with the local KVK before planting.
Source information is included in the offline files; opening source links
requires internet access. Recheck these references before future updates.
